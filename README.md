WHAT IS THIS?
-------------
This folder contains 30 sample B2B (Business to Business) leads structured
as a demonstration of an automated lead generation workflow.

Each lead represents a company decision-maker — CEOs, CFOs, CTOs, VPs, and
Directors — across 10 different industries in the United States.

All contact details are illustrative placeholders (example.com domains,
+1 (example) 555-XXXX phones) and do not represent real individuals or companies.


LEAD DATA FIELDS
----------------
  #               Row number
  Company         Business name
  Domain          Company website domain
  Contact Name    Key decision-maker at the company
  Title           Job title / role
  Industry        Business sector
  Company Size    Employee range (1-50 / 51-200 / 200+)
  Location        City and state (United States)
  Email           Direct contact email
  Phone           Business phone number
  Lead Score      Quality score from 0 to 100
  Status          New / Contacted / Qualified


LEAD SCORING
------------
Each lead has a score from 0 to 100 reflecting its quality and priority:

  Score >= 75   ->  Qualified  (high priority — ready to contact)
  Score 60-74   ->  Contacted  (medium priority — follow up)
  Score <  60   ->  New        (low priority — needs review)

Scoring factors:
  - Seniority of the contact (C-level and VP roles score higher)
  - Company size (larger organizations score higher)
  - Data completeness (email + phone + title all present)
